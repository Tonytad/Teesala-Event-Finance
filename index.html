Common questions

How early should I start planning for retirement?

As early as you can. Even small monthly savings grow a lot over 20 to 30 years, and the planner above shows the effect.

How much should I save before buying a home?

It depends on the home price, your income and the loan terms. We build a deposit target and a repayment you can live with.

Do I need a lot of money to begin?

No. Plans start from the amount you can comfortably set aside each month, and grow as your income does.

What happens in the first consultation?

We talk through your income, goals and concerns. You leave with a clear first step and no obligation.
